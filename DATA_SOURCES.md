Michigan uses location means at 4.05 ng/L after treating `<2` as zero. Minnesota uses maximum PFOA/PFOS at 4 ng/L and excludes purchased-water rows; minimum values provide sensitivity checks. New Hampshire flags describe raw-water sources and support discrimination checks. These endpoints are related to, but not identical with, the national UCMR5 endpoint. Source agencies do not endorse the analysis.

## 31 October strengthening data

Washington's [official PFAS dashboard](https://doh.wa.gov/data-and-statistical-reports/washington-tracking-network-wtn/pfas/dashboard) supplies the downloadable analyte CSV and dictionary. The official Group A general and source inventories supply exact WSID/SrcNum and source classes. Four file URLs, snapshot sizes and SHA256 hashes are recorded in [washington_sources.json](strengthening/washington_sources.json); raw files stay local under `external_raw/wa_doh_20261031/`. The general/source workbooks contain unused public contact columns and are not redistributed. EPA's [June2024 state dataset summaries](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2024-04/cws-service-area-boundaries-state-dataset-summaries.pdf), Washington p72, explicitly documents adding `WA53` to `WS_ID` for the SDWIS crosswalk.

The new source-verified external protocol excludes all 10,313 UCMR5 member IDs before both counting outcomes and evaluating scores. It re-censors numeric concentrations below 4 ng/L to zero and selects fixed count/calendar observation sets rather than averaging unrestricted historical samples. Washington public concentrations have integer display precision without a located rounding rule; additional same-name PFOS records without action-level metadata are resolved only by explicit sensitivity scenarios. The analysis does not infer an isomer identity or an exact annual compliance outcome from those fields. Full endpoint, source-class, follow-up-selection and uncertainty definitions are in [STRENGTHENING.md](STRENGTHENING.md), which supersedes the earlier unrestricted state checks for current reporting.
